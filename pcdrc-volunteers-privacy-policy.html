Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

PCDRC VOLUNTEERS is a mobile application for the Punjab Civil Defence Resilience Corps volunteer management workflow. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information when you use its features:

  • Account details such as CNIC, phone number, email address, password credentials, and assigned role.
  • Volunteer registration details such as name, father or husband name, date of birth, gender, district, address, education, blood group, emergency contact, and application status.
  • Images uploaded for registration, including profile photo and CNIC document images.
  • Course, enrollment, certificate, notification, and report-related records.
  • Basic technical information required for secure API communication and app operation.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate the volunteer management system, including:

  • Creating and authenticating user accounts.
  • Submitting, reviewing, approving, or rejecting volunteer applications.
  • Managing courses, enrollments, training certificates, and reports.
  • Sending service notifications and official broadcast messages.
  • Protecting the security and reliability of the application.

Data Sharing

The app communicates with the official PCDRC backend services. Information may be accessed by authorized Civil Defence, CDO, Executive, and Admin users according to their assigned roles. We do not sell personal information.

Permissions

The app may request access to device media or camera-related files only when a user chooses to upload required registration images. Internet access is required to connect with the backend API.

Data Retention

Registration, course, certificate, and account records are retained as required for official Civil Defence operations, audit, verification, and reporting. Users may request correction or deletion where allowed by applicable rules and system policy.

Security

The app uses authenticated API requests and stores local session information only as needed to keep the user signed in. Users should keep their password confidential and log out from shared devices.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for official volunteer registration and management use. Users should provide accurate information and comply with the eligibility requirements of the PCDRC program.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, backend services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published with a new last updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, data correction, or deletion requests, contact the app publisher through the Google Play Store listing or the official Punjab Civil Defence/Home Department support channel associated with the PCDRC portal.
