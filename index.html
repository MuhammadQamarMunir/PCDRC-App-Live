Privacy Policy

PCDRC ACTIVITIES

App name: PCDRC ACTIVITIES
Last updated: July 20, 2026
Privacy contact: managermis.home@gmail.com

Overview

PCDRC ACTIVITIES is an official operational application used for Civil Defence activity reporting, flood-related monitoring, emergency alerts, profile management, and coordination between authorized users and administrative teams.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information when required for app functionality:

  • Name, email address, phone number, role, district, region, and profile information.
  • Login credentials and authentication tokens used to keep authorized users signed in.
  • Flood activity reports, relief camp details, alert acknowledgements, and submitted form data.
  • Device location coordinates when users capture or update flood activity and camp locations.
  • Photos or uploaded images used as evidence for profile, relief, camp, or activity reporting.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to authenticate users, manage access by role, submit official reports, display dashboards, send and receive emergency alerts, verify field locations, maintain operational records, and support Civil Defence coordination.

Location Data

The app may request location permission to capture the position of flood activities, relief camps, distribution points, or field reporting locations. Location data is used only for official reporting, verification, and operational coordination.

Photos and Uploaded Files

Users may upload images as supporting evidence for official reports or profile updates. Uploaded files are used for verification and record keeping by authorized personnel.

Data Sharing

Data may be shared with authorized Home Department, Civil Defence, district administration, or other approved government users for official operational purposes. We do not sell user data.

Data Security

Data is transmitted to backend services over network connections and is handled using reasonable technical and administrative safeguards designed to protect it from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained for as long as required for official operations, legal obligations, audit records, and administrative purposes. Users may request account or data deletion by contacting managermis.home@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and processed according to applicable official record retention requirements.

Children

This app is intended for authorized official users and is not directed to children.

Advertising

PCDRC ACTIVITIES does not contain third-party advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, data handling, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact: managermis.home@gmail.com.
