How to Request Account Deletion

Users of the PCDRC VOLUNTEERS app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting the privacy support email below.

  1. Send an email to managermis.home@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: PCDRC VOLUNTEERS Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered CNIC, phone number, full name, and district so the account can be verified.
  4. After verification, the request will be reviewed and processed according to official record retention requirements.

Data That May Be Deleted

After a valid deletion request is approved, the following data may be deleted or deactivated where permitted:

  • App account access credentials and active login/session records.
  • Profile details associated with the volunteer account.
  • Uploaded profile photo and CNIC images, where deletion is allowed by official policy.
  • Non-essential app notification and account usage records.

Data That May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when required for official Civil Defence operations, audit, verification, legal obligations, certificate validation, reporting, or administrative purposes. This may include:

  • Submitted volunteer registration and approval/rejection history.
  • Course enrollment, training completion, and certificate records.
  • Administrative review, audit, and official reporting records.
  • Records required by applicable government retention rules.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are reviewed after identity verification. Processing time may vary depending on verification and official record retention requirements.

Request Data Deletion Without Deleting Account

Users may also request correction or deletion of some personal data without deleting their full account by emailing managermis.home@gmail.com with the details of the data they want corrected or deleted.

Important: For security, deletion requests must be sent from or clearly identify the account owner. We may request additional information to verify the user before processing the request.
