Overview

PCDRC VOLUNTEERS is an official mobile application for Punjab Civil Defence Resilience Corps volunteer registration and management. The app is used by volunteers and authorized officers for registration, verification, course enrollment, certificates, notifications, reporting, and official role-based workflows.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information when required for app functionality:

  • Account details such as CNIC, phone number, email address, password credentials, OTP verification data, and assigned role.
  • Volunteer registration details such as name, father or husband name, date of birth, gender, district, address, education, blood group, emergency contact, and application status.
  • Profile photo and CNIC document images selected and uploaded by the user during registration or verification workflows.
  • Course enrollment, training completion, certificate, notification, broadcast, and report-related records.
  • Basic technical information required for authenticated API communication, app security, and reliable service operation.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create, verify, and authenticate user accounts.
  • Submit, review, approve, reject, or manage volunteer applications.
  • Manage courses, enrollments, training completion, and certificates.
  • Send service notifications, official broadcast messages, and account-related OTP messages.
  • Provide dashboards, reports, and role-based access for authorized Civil Defence users.
  • Protect the security, integrity, and reliability of the application and backend services.

Permissions and Uploaded Files

The app requires internet access to communicate with official backend services. The app may allow users to choose profile or CNIC images from their device for registration and verification. Uploaded files are used only for official volunteer management, verification, and record keeping.

Data Sharing

Information may be accessed by authorized Civil Defence, CDO, Executive, Admin, and approved government users according to assigned roles and official operational requirements. We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

The app uses authenticated API requests and stores local session information only as needed to keep authorized users signed in. Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss.

Data Retention and Deletion

Registration, account, course, certificate, notification, and report records are retained as required for official Civil Defence operations, audit, verification, legal obligations, and administrative purposes. Users may request account or data correction or deletion by contacting managermis.home@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and processed according to applicable official record retention requirements.

Children

This app is intended for official volunteer registration and authorized administrative use. It is not directed to children.

Advertising

PCDRC VOLUNTEERS does not contain third-party advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, data handling, backend services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, correction requests, or deletion requests, contact: managermis.home@gmail.com.
